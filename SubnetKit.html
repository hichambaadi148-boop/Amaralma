Privacy Policy

SubnetKit for Android · Developed by Amar Alma LLC
Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the SubnetKit mobile app (the "App"), developed and published by Amar Alma LLC ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use it. SubnetKit is designed to work on your device without collecting your personal data.

In short
  • We do not collect your personal information through the App.
  • No account, advertising or analytics services.
  • Notes you write stay on your device.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

2. Information we collect

None. SubnetKit does not require registration and does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or device identifiers. The App does not include analytics, advertising or tracking services, and it does not send the content you enter to any server.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Notes: the titles, text and color of notes you create are saved locally in the App's private storage on your device.
  • Tool input: values you type into the calculators and tools (such as IP addresses, JSON, Base64 or URL text) are processed on your device only. We do not receive or store them.

4. Clipboard

When you tap a copy button, the App places the selected result or note on your clipboard. The App does not read your clipboard.

5. Permissions

SubnetKit does not request access to sensitive data such as location, contacts, files, camera or microphone.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise share personal information with third parties. Because the App does not collect personal data, we hold none from the App that could be disclosed.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your notes remain on your device until you delete them. You can remove a note with its Delete button, clear all App data in Android Settings → Apps → SubnetKit → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App. We keep no copy of your notes on our servers, so we cannot retrieve or delete them for you.

If you contact us by email, we keep your message only as long as needed to respond to you, and you may ask us to delete it at any time.

8. Backups

If backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up App data (including your notes) to your Google Account. This is controlled by your device settings and handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy, not by us.

9. Google Play and external links

If you choose to share diagnostics data with Google, Google may provide us with aggregated crash and performance reports through Google Play. These reports do not identify you to us.

The App contains an optional link to this Privacy Policy. When you open it, your browser connects to our website, which may process standard server logs (such as IP address and time of access) for security and operation.

10. Security

Your notes are stored in Android's private app storage, which is protected by the Android security model. No method of storage is completely secure, so please do not save passwords or other sensitive secrets in your notes.

11. Children's privacy

SubnetKit is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because the App does not collect personal data, there is none for us to provide, correct or delete. If you have a question or request, contact us using the details below.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, and the "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
